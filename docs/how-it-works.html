Step by step

Start with the perspective that fits your work.

Readiness looks different across an institution. HEAIR adapts the assessment to the responsibilities of the person completing it.

1

Select your stakeholder role

Choose the role that best reflects your current responsibilities. The survey then uses questions relevant to that role, rather than asking every person the same generic questions.

StudentFacultyLeadershipBusiness AffairsIT Staff
2

Complete your 12-question assessment

You answer 12 role-specific questions across the four HEAIR readiness dimensions. There are no trick questions. Choose the response that best reflects your current experience or environment.

Governance & StrategySystems & InfrastructureCultureEducation
Your results

Understand the score before deciding what to do next.

Your responses are normalized into an overall AI-readiness score, a readiness level, and four dimension scores. The result gives you a clear picture without pretending that one number tells the whole story.

3

What your report shows

✓How your overall score was calculated from the 12 responses.
✓What your readiness level means in practical terms.
✓Which HEAIR dimensions increased or lowered your score.
✓Your strongest areas to build on and weakest areas to improve first.
✓How your score compares with your stakeholder group at your university when enough completed results are available.
Learn more about my score

Open a conversation about your results.

The option on your report opens a full-screen coaching chat. It carries your role, score profile, strengths, and priority areas into the conversation.

What should I focus on first?
Your coach explains the first practical move based on your score profile and the HEAIR guidance retrieved for it.
University comparison

See your score in the context of your university.

When enough people in your stakeholder group at the same university have completed the assessment, your report adds a university comparison directly above the dimension scores.

What the comparison shows

It helps you see whether your latest score is above, below, or in line with others who share your role at your university.

Overall readinessYour score alongside the university average for your stakeholder group.
Four readiness dimensionsYour Governance, Systems, Culture, and Education scores compared with the group average.
Current and fairEach account contributes its latest completed assessment, so repeated attempts do not change the comparison.
Private by design

People are never identified.

The comparison appears only after enough completed results are available. It does not show names, emails, individual reports, or rankings.

Evidence-informed guidance

Receive your AI-powered roadmap.

The AI does not create recommendations from the score alone. Your score identifies readiness gaps; the RAG knowledge base retrieves relevant sections of the HEAIR research paper and approved AI-readiness resources; then the AI uses that evidence to create role-specific guidance.

YOUR PROFILEScore identifies the gaps

Your role, responses, overall score, dimension scores, and lowest-scoring areas set the focus.

RAG KNOWLEDGE BASEResearch supplies context

Relevant HEAIR evidence and approved resources are retrieved for the profile before advice is written.

AI ROADMAPGuidance becomes actionable

You receive personalized recommendations, priorities, next steps, longer-term goals, and useful policies, training, tools, or resources.

Important: Recommendations are guidance, not final institutional decisions. Review them with your institution’s policies, experts, and local context before acting on them.

Source support: Recommendations are grounded in the HEAIR framework paper and retrieved approved AI-readiness resources. Source references should accompany the guidance so you can understand the evidence behind a recommendation.
