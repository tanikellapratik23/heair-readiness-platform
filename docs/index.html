How this AI coach uses your information

It uses this saved assessment, your selected role, university, optional unit, and relevant HEAIR source material. It does not browse the internet in this experience. Conversations are saved to your account until you delete them or an approved institutional retention policy changes. Recommendations can be wrong and should be reviewed by institutional stakeholders.
